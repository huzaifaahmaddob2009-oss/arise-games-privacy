Privacy Policy

Arrow Pin Puzzle 3D, by Arise Games. Last updated: November 2026.

This policy explains what information the game Arrow Pin Puzzle 3D (the “App”) uses. Arise Games (“we”, “us”) does not run any servers for this App and does not ask you to create an account.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

The App saves your game data (current level, stars, coins, items you bought, settings and daily challenge progress) only on your device. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this saved data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the EU and UK), the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options (ads). You can also reset or turn off personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded ads are optional. You only receive a reward if you watch the ad to the end.

3. Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases (such as coin packs), they are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help.

5. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google (for ads and, if enabled, purchases), as described above. Because your game data stays on your device, protecting your device (screen lock, updates) also protects your game data.

6. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options (ads).
  • Delete all game data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it.
  • Limit ad personalization in your Android Google settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
